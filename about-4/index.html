Our Story

20 Years of Experience

With 20 years of experience as a leading property management company, our managed corporate housing system consistently delivers high satisfaction for both owners and tenants. We recognize the diverse investment property options available and value your home as a significant asset. By attentively listening to your needs and guiding you through the process, we ensure your investment is both straightforward and profitable.

Our property management office operates Monday through Friday, staffed with dedicated professionals ready to answer your questions, handle maintenance requests, and maintain your property to the highest standards.

For Owners

Hands-Off Ownership

Like our furnished property management program, WCH offers a full-service program for managing your property as an unfurnished rental in most of our local markets. We handle all aspects of the rental process, including finding and screening tenants, marketing your property, and negotiating with tenants to maintain high occupancy rates.

Owners can expect a hands-off service, meaning we take care of everything, so you don't have to.
